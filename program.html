Faculty model

Built like a teaching hospital, not a high school.

Students would rather learn from people actively solving today's problems than from someone who left the field years ago. So we don't hire teachers first — we recruit the best practitioners in Oklahoma and put them in front of students.

3–5 full-time coaches

The spine of the academy: master tradespeople and GiANT-certified coaches who own each student's development arc and the dashboard behind it.

25–40 adjunct experts

The best service manager, field trainer, dispatcher, estimator, and ops leader in the market — each teaching a day every week or two, straight from the field.

100 contractor mentors

Ride-alongs, internships, and mentorship from the employer network. Students constantly interact with practitioners still in the business.
